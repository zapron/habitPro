## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Most habit apps bet everything on willpower — the first thing to run out
when you're doing it alone. HabitPro is built around missions you do WITH
other people, so the hardest days are never days you face by yourself.

WHY HABITPRO IS DIFFERENT
Any app can track a checkmark. HabitPro notices when you go quiet, and gives
your squad a way to catch you before a bad week turns into a broken streak.

MAIN MISSIONS
Run 7, 21, or 30-day missions with streak grids, XP, finish reports, and
streak repair when momentum needs backup.

MINI MISSIONS
Quick focus timers for the small stuff that still matters — water, walking,
reading, cleanup, or a hard 15-minute push. Add a photo or note to keep the
proof honest.

LIVE SQUAD
Invite people into a mini mission together. Everyone picks their own timer;
the board ranks who locked their finish first. Async pressure, without
forcing everyone online at once.

COMMUNITY
Cheer wins, join group missions, publish moments, send nudges, and climb the
weekly XP leaderboard. Make effort visible to the people who'll actually
notice.

FREE TO START
The full solo habit loop — main missions, mini missions, streak grids, XP —
is free, forever. HabitPro Community adds squads, group pressure, streak
repairs, and public momentum.

Built for: accountability partners, habit tracking with friends, streak
tracking, focus timers, goal tracking, and anyone whose habits keep breaking
the moment life gets busy.
```
